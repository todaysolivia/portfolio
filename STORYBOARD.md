# Storyboard: "Curious like a cat"

## Central idea

> **Curiosity didn't kill this cat. It made her a designer.**

Olivia designs the way a cat explores: curious, playful, bold, and happy to try again. A black cat is her alter ego and a pink sparkle is an idea.
Every section fills the whole screen, and every move between sections is something a cat actually does.

## Inspiration
- **Valorant's Evori Dreamwings collection:** magical-girl companions (including Lunari the cat) that float and twinkle, with iridescent wings and sparkles. These drive the winged familiar cat and the glowing butterflies in "lights out".
- **Olivia's moodboard:** the *Bold Wister* poster and the Snow → Rich Mahogany palette card.

## Design rules
- **Full bleed:** every section is the full screen, with no boxes, frames or rounded cards.
- **Type is the layout:** giant Caprasimo words, elegant Melodrama lines, small Apfel Grotezk text.
- **Colour blocks:** each section is one colour from her palette (poster pink, mahogany, black, petal, electric rose), with hard cuts between them.
- **Always alive:** a black paw cursor (white outline, visible on every colour), eyes that follow it, a tail that sways, letters you can bat.
- **Always know where you are:** a progress line along the bottom with a stop for each section. Drag the little cat along it to move through the site.
